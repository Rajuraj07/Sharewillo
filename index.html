ShareWillow helps you in a few easy steps

1.

Determine your cadence

Choose how frequently you want to pay out cash bonuses. We recommend you do it every quarter to build a positive feedback loop, but have a 30-60 day delay in payout so you can close all your books.

Learn more

2.

Set up thresholds

Set company-wide goals that have to be hit in order for your incentive plan to payout. You’ll give your team something to work towards, and make sure a payout only happens when you can afford it.

Learn more

3.

Establish formulas

Set up formulas for different employees. You can determinea minimum amount of tenure to participate in the program, group employees together by role, and customize it any way you want.

4.

Launch and share

Invite your employees into ShareWillow where they can see their calculations, goals, and documents in one dashboard.
